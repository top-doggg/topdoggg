# Pride In My Community

## Three Stories, One Drop

### Presented by TRST Studios / DE.LA.COSTA

## The Idea

Pride In My Community is an artist-led visual and apparel campaign about the people who keep a neighborhood moving. It is not a generic product shoot. It is a three-part portrait series built around courage, connection, and daily resilience.

Each story anchors one made-to-order TRST Studios tee:

| Story | Piece | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connection | Better Together Tee | Community over isolation. |
| Courage | Sin Miedo Tee | Move without fear. |
| Resilience | No Bad Days Tee | Everyday momentum through real pressure. |

## What Gets Made

- Three short vertical portrait films for social distribution
- A photo series and campaign artwork
- One campaign landing page and QR-led release path
- A made-to-order apparel drop through secure checkout
- A documented campaign recap for partners

## Why Partner

Partners help bring a real community story into view without forcing the work into a conventional advertisement. The campaign creates cultural visibility, useful content, and a tangible made-to-order release without an inventory gamble.

## Partnership Lanes

### Presenting Partner

Underwrites the complete three-story campaign and is credited as the presenting supporter across agreed launch materials and recap content.

### Story Partner

Supports one portrait, one community moment, or one release element that aligns naturally with their work.

### In-Kind Partner

Contributes space, production support, food, sound, styling, talent access, promotion, or other resources that make the work possible.

## What Partners Receive

- A respectful role in an original community-facing project
- Agreed presenting or supporting credit
- Approved campaign images or video clips for their channels
- QR-led connection to the campaign and product release
- A post-launch recap covering agreed activity and outcomes

## How We Protect the Work

- Partner placement is designed around the story, never dropped into it as generic advertising.
- TRST and the featured people retain creative approval over the final work.
- Deliverables, timing, logo treatment, content permissions, and any contribution are agreed in writing before production begins.
- A campaign is only launched when the partner fit is real.

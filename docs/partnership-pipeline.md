# TRST Studios Partnership Pipeline

## Objective

Move DE.LA.COSTA from Printify-led direct sales into a retail-ready apparel operation, then use creator-led advertising to establish demand before pitching major retailers.

## Positioning

- Brand: TRST Studios / DE.LA.COSTA
- Product: artist-led graphic apparel rooted in community, place, cultural memory, and visual storytelling
- Current channel: direct-to-consumer sales through the TRST storefront and Printify Pop-Up Store checkout
- Constraint: Printify is suitable for direct-to-consumer fulfillment but does not create a dependable wholesale inventory program for retail purchase orders.

## Selected Opening Capsule

1. Better Together Tee
2. Sin Miedo Tee
3. No Bad Days Tee

## Advertising Plan

### Stage 1: Creator seeding

Recruit 15-20 micro-creators whose work visibly overlaps with streetwear, art, music, skating, tattoo culture, or community storytelling. Prioritize audience fit and video quality over follower count.

- Offer: gifted item plus a tracked commission on completed sales.
- Deliverable: one short-form video, three usable photos, and a tracked storefront link.
- Tracking: unique `utm_source=creator` links and creator-specific discount codes.
- Decision rule: continue only with creators who generate product-page visits, email signups, or checkout starts.

### Stage 2: Paid amplification

Use the strongest creator video as a small Meta and TikTok test. Do not begin with polished static ads.

- Test one product at a time.
- Optimize first for product-detail views and checkout starts, then purchases after enough conversion data exists.
- Keep the initial budget capped until one creative produces repeatable qualified traffic.

### Stage 3: Retail proof

Save the verified results: creator posts, site sessions, subscriber growth, checkout starts, customer photos, and sell-through by design. These become the proof section for a Culture Kings buyer pitch.

## What Must Exist Before Wholesale Outreach

1. Three physical samples with final garment, print, neck-label, and packaging quality approved.
2. Cost sheet for 100, 250, and 500 units per style.
3. MSRP, wholesale price, MOQ, lead time, size run, and payment terms.
4. UPCs or retailer-assigned barcodes, carton details, and clear return/defect process.
5. Final buyer deck at `/wholesale`, plus photography of physical samples.

## Recommended First Move

Request matched sample and bulk quotes from Wearlets and Minku for the Better Together Tee, Sin Miedo Tee, and No Bad Days Tee. Choose the producer only after comparing sample quality, landed unit cost, production lead time, print durability, packaging, and payment terms.

# TRST collector CRM and marketing system

## Relationship types

Every meaningful contact belongs to one primary relationship type: collector, curator, gallery, designer, cultural organization, collaborator, or warm contact. The private `/operations` desk derives these categories from incoming artwork and partnership inquiries and provides a CSV export for backup or migration.

## Minimum contact record

- Name, email, organization, relationship type
- Source and campaign attribution
- Artwork(s) of interest
- Inquiry type and permission/consent status
- Pipeline stage: new, approved, follow-up, declined, published
- Last contact date, next action date, private notes
- Touchpoint history and owner

## Collector journey

1. **Work:** a visitor sees all ten works and filters by series.
2. **Story:** each detail view explains the work before asking for contact information.
3. **Artist:** the practice is positioned through place, cultural memory, and community authorship.
4. **Available work:** availability is honest and inquiry-led until physical metadata and prices are confirmed.
5. **Inquiry:** the record is saved privately and appears in the operations pipeline.
6. **Follow-up:** the studio responds personally, qualifies intent, and records the next action.

## Weekly operating rhythm

- Monday: choose one artwork and one audience segment.
- Tuesday: publish the work’s story with one direct link to its portfolio record.
- Wednesday: send five personal notes to relevant warm contacts; never mass-pitch.
- Thursday: follow up on open inquiries and update pipeline stages.
- Friday: share process, context, or community connection—not another sales post.
- Monthly: review inquiry sources, strongest series, response time, qualified conversations, and collector conversions.

## Campaign structure

Use one artwork per campaign week. Build three assets from the same record: the full image, a detail/crop, and a short artist note. Route every post to the artwork detail—not the shop homepage. Use campaign tags such as `utm_source=instagram`, `utm_medium=organic`, `utm_campaign=dlc_001`, and a content tag describing the asset.

Success is measured by qualified inquiries, curator/gallery conversations, repeat visits, replies from warm contacts, and completed acquisitions—not raw reach alone.

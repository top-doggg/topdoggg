# TRST Studios Production Quote Request

## Subject

Quote request: three-style graphic tee capsule / samples and bulk production

## Message

Hello,

I represent TRST Studios / DE.LA.COSTA, an independent art and apparel label preparing a first wholesale-ready graphic tee capsule. We are looking for a production partner that can support approved samples first, followed by a small bulk run.

The opening capsule includes:

1. Better Together Tee
2. Sin Miedo Tee
3. No Bad Days Tee

Please quote the following for each style:

- One pre-production sample per style
- Bulk production at 100, 250, and 500 total units per style
- Heavyweight unisex tee options and garment specifications
- Screen-print pricing by print-location and ink-color count
- Woven neck label, size label, and inside-care-label options
- Fold, polybag, and individual barcode-label options
- Production lead time after artwork and sample approval
- Shipping terms and estimated delivery to the United States
- Payment schedule, sample revision policy, and defect/reprint policy

Please also send your available blank-garment options, sample pricing, MOQ requirements, print-area specifications, and recent examples of comparable graphic tees.

We can supply final print-ready artwork and placement files after an initial capability and pricing review.

Thank you,

TRST Studios / DE.LA.COSTA

## Internal Comparison Rules

Do not choose a producer from price alone. Score each quote on:

1. Sample quality and print durability
2. Landed unit cost at 100 and 250 units
3. Garment weight, fit, and consistency
4. Lead time and communication quality
5. Labeling, packaging, and barcode capability
6. Deposit terms and defect-resolution policy

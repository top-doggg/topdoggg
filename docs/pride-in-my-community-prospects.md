# Pride In My Community: First Partner List

Campaign: **Pride In My Community: Three Stories, One Drop**  
Capsule: **Better Together**, **Sin Miedo**, and **No Bad Days**

This is an outreach map, not a claim that any organization has agreed to sponsor TRST. Start with the `Now` group. The ask should be a short conversation about one defined collaboration, not a request for an open-ended donation.

## Message Rule

Lead with one sentence about their specific work, then the **one collaboration** being proposed. Include the campaign brief and a small proof pack: product mockups, one founder photo, store link, and the planned community outcome. Do not lead with fundraising language or an investor pitch.

## Research Notes

- The LAB publishes recurring markets, artist features, pop-ups, and community events, making it the clearest near-term OC activation lead.
- OPEN LA explicitly presents independent fashion, music, art, and community events, and publicly invites vendors, artists, and brands.
- Oakland Museum of California publicly describes corporate partnerships and custom marketing opportunities; this supports a tailored partnership inquiry, not an assumption of availability or approval.
- Public-sector and nonprofit organizations may be better connectors or program partners than direct commercial sponsors. Treat them as relationship-building routes, not guaranteed funding sources.

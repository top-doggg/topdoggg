# TRST Site Forge Marketing And Sales Plan

## Offer

TRST Site Forge sells a simple monthly website service for small brands, artists, local shops, creators, and service businesses.

Core promise: upload the vibe, approve the blueprint, launch the site, keep it cared for.

Plans:

- Starter Care: $29/mo for hosted presence, light monthly updates, and launch support.
- Growth Care: $59/mo for lead capture, analytics review, managed updates, and seasonal refreshes.
- Studio Care: $99/mo for campaign pages, product drops, priority changes, and hands-on creative direction.

## Positioning

Most AI website builders sell speed. TRST should sell taste plus continuity.

The angle:

- AI-assisted blueprinting lowers cost and speeds up the first draft.
- Human creative review keeps the result from feeling generic.
- Monthly care keeps the client from being abandoned after launch.
- Social/photo intake makes the process easier for clients who do not have a formal brand kit.

## Acquisition Flow

1. Website path: `trststudios.online/site-forge`
   - Prospects pick a plan.
   - They enter client details, social links, images, style words, and prompt.
   - The blueprint is saved privately for review.

2. Social campaign path:
   - Instagram/Facebook posts show before/after blueprint thinking.
   - Story CTA: "Send your vibe. Get a site blueprint."
   - Link points to `/site-forge`.

3. Direct outreach path:
   - Target small local businesses with outdated sites, no clear lead form, or strong social content but weak web presence.
   - Offer a free first blueprint review, then monthly plan activation.

4. Referral path:
   - Existing shirt/community audience gets a simple referral offer.
   - Reward can be a discount, free refresh, or merch credit.

## Retention Flow

Monthly client rhythm:

- Week 1: check form submissions, site health, and needed content changes.
- Week 2: publish one update, landing block, product/drop edit, or gallery refresh.
- Week 3: send a plain-language performance note.
- Week 4: plan next month?s campaign, offer, or seasonal update.

Retention promise: the site keeps moving with the business.

## Sales Pipeline

Stages:

1. New intake
2. Qualified fit
3. Blueprint sent
4. Plan selected
5. Build in progress
6. Live client
7. Monthly care
8. At risk / save motion

Qualification rules:

- Strong fit: has visual material, clear offer, reachable owner, and recurring update need.
- Medium fit: needs basic presence and lead capture, but limited assets.
- Weak fit: wants full custom app, no budget, or no clear owner.

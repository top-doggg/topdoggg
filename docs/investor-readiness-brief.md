# TRST Studios Investor Readiness Brief

## Purpose

Prepare a private, factual conversation brief for warm investor introductions. This is not an offer to sell securities, and it must not state valuation, ownership, return, or fundraising terms until reviewed with qualified legal and financial advisors.

## Public Versus Private

- Public website: brand story, sponsorship opportunities, strategic partnerships, work samples, and contact intake.
- Private investor conversation: traction, operating plan, use of funds, financial projections, risks, and any proposed terms.
- Do not publish a public investment ask, valuation, ownership percentage, or expected returns on the TRST website.

## The Story

TRST Studios is building an independent art, apparel, and visual-storytelling label around DE.LA.COSTA. The model begins with made-to-order direct sales, creates cultural proof through partnerships and artist-led releases, and graduates proven designs into selectively funded wholesale production.

## Evidence To Collect

1. Monthly website visitors, product-detail views, checkout starts, and completed orders.
2. Revenue, gross margin, refunds, and repeat-customer rate by product.
3. Email-list growth and conversion rate from subscriber to buyer.
4. Creator and partner campaign results: reach, tracked sessions, sales, and content rights secured.
5. Production quotes for 100, 250, and 500 units of the three-style opening capsule.
6. A cash-flow plan showing exactly what outside capital would fund and how it creates a measurable next milestone.

## Investor Conversation Outline

1. The cultural and commercial problem TRST solves.
2. The product, direct-to-customer model, and production strategy.
3. Evidence of demand and the three leading products.
4. Partnership and distribution strategy.
5. Capital need and milestone-based use of funds.
6. Operating risks: product demand, lead time, margin, returns, and concentration of channels.
7. Team, ownership, and legal structure.

## Sponsor Conversation Outline

1. The story or activation being supported.
2. The people and community it reaches.
3. What the sponsor receives: agreed credit, content, activation presence, or documented community outcome.
4. Timeline, budget, and deliverables.
5. Brand-safety and approval process.
6. Post-campaign recap metrics.

## Compliance Note

In the U.S., a public website can be viewed as general solicitation when it markets an offering of securities. The SEC notes that private-placement pathways can restrict general solicitation, while public investment offerings require a specific compliant pathway. Obtain legal advice before discussing investment terms or accepting investment money.

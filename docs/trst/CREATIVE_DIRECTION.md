# TRST Studios — creative handoff

## Identity
TRST Studios, De.La.Costa. Artist-led experimental typography, Chicano/neighborhood storytelling, street-rooted sophistication. Art carries themes of public appearance versus inner emotion, community experience, resilience, ritual and identity.

## Approved shirt direction
Black and white tees use blue-and-ivory back illustration (smiling mask, crying face/teardrop, watchful eye, broken heart, curving structural motifs). Small chrome-blue TRST/studios.online left-chest pocket print. Keep artwork, typography, placement and scale as supplied. Use high-resolution source assets rather than reconstructed generative graphics. No AI mockup is automatically a validated print master.

## Graphite heart direction
Two expressive adult female faces and one central symbolic eye, connected by smoke/ribbons and geometric spikes. The entire **OUTER SILHOUETTE IS A HEART**, including unmistakable two upper lobes and tapered bottom. No replacement with a long vertical crest or small heart pendant. Real pencil visual character: line weight variation, erasure, overlapping exploratory marks, smudged shading, believable anatomy, natural paper tooth. Monochrome, no glossy chrome.

## TRST drop campaign
“What they see / what you carry.” Raw Southern California barrio neighborhood realism without harmful stereotypes. Candid adults by an old coupe washing a car, friends talking by open windows, hanging out with dog, 2000s video/home-camera sense. These are mood references, not proof or license to recreate identifiable people. Story has linear geographic continuity: neighborhood → people wearing exact shirts → artwork details → drop reveal. Intentional hard cuts, crisp shots and minimal titles; avoid fades, artificial distortions and blurry printed logos.

## Quality gates
Compare edited product visuals side-by-side with source at native resolution; preserve exact details. For video check clothing consistency and character anatomy every cut, avoid generative logo morphing, use original still artwork for sharp hero shots, use 9:16 for reels. Confirm ownership/consent for photography and release timing before public launch.
